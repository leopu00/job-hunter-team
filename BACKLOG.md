## 🔁 Repository hygiene

- 🔴 **[BRANCH-LIFECYCLE-CLEANUP]** — la chiusura di un merge deve essere meccanica e nello stesso giro: dopo push verde e `worktree-sync` verificato `0/0 clean`, HQ-MASTER elimina il branch remoto se l'owner non deve proseguire sullo stesso fronte; quando la sessione viene ritirata rimuove anche la worktree pulita e il branch locale già raggiungibile da `master`. A ogni giro di integrazione esegue inoltre un census fail-closed (`git branch -r` + `git worktree list`) che segnala: branch merge-ready non integrate, ref remote già antenate di `master`, e worktree senza sessione. Nessun branch con commit unici si elimina senza decisione esplicita merge/abbandono e verifica del contenuto.
- 🔴 **[CROSS-BOUNDARY-CONTRACT-FREEZE]** — quando due owner producono e consumano lo stesso artefatto o protocollo, prima di scrivere codice devono congelare una specifica unica e versionata: nomi file, schema e tipi esatti, valori ammessi, canonicalizzazione, limiti, semantica fail-closed e vettori di test condivisi. Producer e consumer implementano e revisionano contro quella stessa fonte; varianti concorrenti non entrano nei branch e ogni modifica al contratto richiede una correzione esplicita che supersede le precedenti prima di riprendere il lavoro.

> `[QUEUE-NO-DEFAULT-LIMIT]` è stato **chiuso per implementazione** il 2026-07-30. Le undici code hanno un default di **20 righe** — uguale per tutte, perché una coda di lavoro serve a prendere il prossimo item e il quadro d'insieme ora arriva dal totale, che si vede sempre: `(mostrate 20 di 1375)` più la riga che dice come alzarlo. > `[DEDUP-URL-CORRECTNESS]` è stato **chiuso per implementazione** il 2026-07-30. (a) Il `LIKE` del livello 0 è ora un **prefiltro** su `%/jobs/view/<id>%` e ogni candidato viene **riconfermato** riestraendo l'id dal suo path: `4381470286` e `43814702861` tornano due annunci diversi. - ⬜ **[SOURCE-YIELD-MEMORY]** — the team keeps no memory of how well a *source* performs, so a Scout can work an unproductive lane for hours and nobody notices. Measured by hand on a live team 2026-07-27 (50 positions): the dominant job board returned **62% reaching a score**, while two other lanes returned **0%** — one of them an aggregator that serves expired ads as HTTP 200 with the closed-marker buried in the page's localisation bundle, so the standard link gate cannot discriminate on it. Nothing in the system looks at this: the Mentor reads patterns but advises the *user* about skills, and the Captain assigns lanes by what is **uncovered**, not by what has **paid off**. Note the defence-in-depth worked exactly as designed — Analysts caught every dead link with `recheck-liveness` and the user saw none — so this is not a correctness bug; the cost is the wasted analyses upstream of that catch. **Deliberately not a blocklist**: sources are discovered by exploration, not hardcoded, so banning a domain ages out immediately — the same exploration produced the highest-scoring position of the run. What is missing is the aggregate: yield per source, surfaced where lanes get assigned. Constrained by [SCORE-INTEGRITY-NO-UPSTREAM-FILTER]: yield may reorder **where sourcing starts**, never what is allowed in. **Osservazione dell'utente 2026-07-28, da approfondire prima di implementare**: nel gioco non ha mai visto due Scout lavorare *insieme* — per esempio entrambi su LinkedIn, coordinandosi — e ritiene che sarebbe piu' efficiente della divisione per territori. > `[CODEX-PROLITE-SINGLE-WINDOW]` è stato **chiuso per implementazione** (commit `b0ab36c0c`). `fetch_codex_rollout` ora legge `window_minutes`, già presente nel payload: quando `primary` dura un giorno o più e `secondary` è assente, primary **È** la weekly e viene riportata su entrambi gli assi — il pacer 5h diventa un pacer weekly corretto e SOPRA-PACE/`proj_weekly`/weekly-halt tornano a vedere. Una finestra corta senza secondary lascia weekly `None` invece di inventarla. Layout classico a due finestre invariato; test a tre casi in `tests/test_codex_single_window.py`. Voce originale, per storia: **on the Codex "prolite" plan the entire weekly budget is silently treated as a 5-hour window, and every weekly-based protection is blind.** Root cause found 2026-07-30 by reading the raw rollout `rate_limits` on a live box: `primary = {used_percent: 24, window_minutes: 10080, resets_at: +7d}`, `secondary: null`, `plan_type: "prolite"` — this plan exposes **one** rate-limit window, weekly, inside `primary`. `fetch_codex_rollout()` in `sentinel-bridge.py` assumes the classic layout (primary = short window, secondary = weekly): with `secondary: null` every weekly field lands as `None`, so SOPRA-PACE, `pace_guard`'s weekly axis, `proj_weekly` and the weekly-halt safety never engage — and worse, the 7-day quota flows into `usage`, which all downstream reasoning treats as a 5h window whose saturation means "quiet for a couple of hours". On this plan saturation means **mute for days**. The team was measured running at 1.3× with nothing able to brake it, projected to exhaust on 1-2 August with the reset on the 5th. The fix is one field away: the payload **already carries `window_minutes`** — if `>= 1440`, primary IS the weekly: populate `weekly_usage`/`weekly_reset_at*` from it, flag the short window as absent (never as 0%), and log `plan_type`. Interim mitigations applied by hand: worker throttles raised and the Captain told to treat `usage` as his weekly and pace to 100% no earlier than the reset. Related: the reset-table section in `vps-access/README.md` already documents the symptom from the outside.

Verifica incrociata: sul gradino da 5 minuti con cinque worker la formula dà 60s, cioè esattamente lo scaglionamento che `first-run-burst` aveva tarato a mano. `shared/skills/spawn_stagger.py` (24 test in `tests/test_spawn_stagger.py`), invocato da `.launcher/start-agent.sh`; skill `spawn-agent` aggiornata in tutte e 7 le lingue; interruttore `JHT_SPAWN_STAGGER=0` per gli spawn che non devono essere ritardati. **Residuo noto**: `scaling-calc` porta ancora la ladder vecchia a multipli di 5 (`LADDER = [300, 600, 900, …]`), che non è più quella di `throttle-config` — il suo `wait_after_min` è stato però riqualificato per quello che è, una finestra di osservazione fra due step, non uno sfasamento.

## 🛠️ Infra & CLI

- 🔴 **[JHT-CLI-AGENT-PARITY]** — **the CLI reads and commands, but cannot decide.** The project's stated design rule lives in `docs/guides/AI-AGENT-INTEGRATION.md`: *"If a feature requires opening the web dashboard or the Desktop app to be configured after install, that's a bug — one CLI surface for humans, AI agents and the Desktop launcher."* Measured against the game's `BackendBus` verbs on **2026-07-25**, it is not being met.

  | | 🎮 game | 🔧 CLI |
  |---|---|---|
  | read — positions, state, stats, logs, graphs | ✅ | ✅ `positions list/show`, `stats`, `status`, `logs`, `sentinella graph` |
  | command — start/stop the team, talk to an agent, providers, hours | ✅ | ✅ `team start/stop/send/chat`, `providers`, `working-hours`, `cron` |
  | **decide** — judge a position, exclude it, request a CV, open a ticket, post a standing directive, save the profile, fetch a produced artifact | ✅ | ❌ **nothing** |

  `jht positions` describes itself in code as *"Query DB posizioni (proxy a db_query.py)"* — read-only by construction. The missing verbs are exactly the ones that carry a **user judgement**, which is what the human does in the office and an agent currently cannot do at all.

  The capability is already there, one layer down: `shared/skills/{ticket,team_directives,write_request,feedback_query}.py`. What is missing is the `jht` verb in front — wrapping, not design.

  **The bigger blocker is not the verbs, it is the output format.** Only **3 of 37** command modules support `--json`/`--format` (`container`, `profile`, `tools`). An agent reading `jht positions list` gets an ASCII table meant for a human eye and has to parse it with brittle regexes — while `db_query.py` underneath already knows how to emit JSON and the flag simply is not exposed. Work, in order: (1) `--json` across the read commands; (2) the decision verbs; (3) a CI parity check that fails when the game gains a verb the CLI lacks, so this cannot drift again.

  **Done 2026-07-25 — all three steps.** (1) `--json` on the seven `db_query.py` read commands, surfaced on `jht positions list/show/dashboard`. (2) Decision verbs: `jht positions exclude/restore/request-cv`, `jht ticket open/list/count/show/for-position/assign/resolve`, `jht directives add/list/edit/archive/show`. Two skills had to be written or extended, because the verb existed *only* inside a web route and was therefore browser-only: `shared/skills/user_exclude.py` (new) and `ticket.py open`. (3) `tests/test_cli_game_parity.py` classifies every public `BackendBus` verb as covered / known-gap / not-applicable and **fails on an unclassified one** — verified by injecting a fake verb and watching it name it.

  **Residual, still open:** like/dislike/star feedback (lives only in Supabase `position_feedback`, needs the cloud lane, not SQLite), `fetch_artifact`, `upload_user_document`, and read/write of the coordinator settings. Each is listed in `KNOWN_GAPS` inside the parity test, so the list cannot quietly rot. Also open: `user_exclude.py` and the TS route now hold **two implementations of the same rules** — one test compares their reason sets, nothing compares their behaviour.
